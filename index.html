How to Play Cows & Bulls

Crack the hidden 5-letter secret word (5 unique letters) within 10 guesses. Every guess must be a valid dictionary word.

BULL — Exact Match
The letter is in the secret word AND in the exact same position.
COW — Wrong Position
The letter exists in the secret word, but in a different position.
Example: Secret word is FLAME
If you guess FRAME → 4 Bulls (F, A, M, E in exact spots) & 0 Cows.
If you guess LEMON → 0 Bulls & 3 Cows (L, E, M exist in other spots).
